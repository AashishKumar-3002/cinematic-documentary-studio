# Lessons from the Lake Nyos pilot

Source: the user's direct review of the completed documentary.

The video generally looked good. The user requested that the following be saved as learning for future productions, explicitly without recreating this documentary.

1. The explanation and narration were not engaging enough. Improve scripting and delivery together, including pacing, emotion and the way the story develops.
2. Make the video interact with the narration. Highlight, circle, underline, trace or animate the relevant object or relationship when it is mentioned, so picture and voice guide attention together.
3. Use purposeful effects and sound for scene changes, writing and important moments. Important beats should receive appropriate audio emphasis.

These lessons are incorporated into the documentary-production-playbook skill, referenced from the primary cinematic-film skill and the project production profile. They are future production priorities, not measured audience-retention results. The existing film and its exports remain unchanged.
