# Documentary studio visual direction

## Style Prompt
An investigative science documentary: charcoal canvas, warm ivory editorial headlines, restrained copper accents and precise diagram lines. Slow deliberate camera moves; strong readable composition; diagrams clearly presented as illustrations.

## Colors
- Canvas: #101B20
- Main type: #F4EBDD
- Secondary type: #BBCDD0
- Accent: #E9AC77
- Water/diagram: #477F89

## Typography
Georgia bold for editorial headlines; Courier New for small technical labels. Use installed system fonts for the setup test.

## Motion
0.7 second entrances; smooth easing; gentle diagram movement. 1920 by 1080, 30 fps. Keep key text inside the central 80 percent.

## What NOT to Do
- No fake archive evidence.
- No graphic depictions of victims.
- No busy wall of competing labels.
- No paid media generation without explicit budget authorization.
