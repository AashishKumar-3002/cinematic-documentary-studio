# Atmospheric scene direction

Treat this as a creative reference, not a required style or production method.

## Scene direction

- Establish the place and scale before focusing on a meaningful detail.
- Separate foreground, subject and distant atmosphere to make depth readable.
- Choose a coherent lighting palette and preserve a clear subject silhouette.
- Build anticipation through details, reveal the consequential action, then allow an aftermath when the story benefits.
- Adapt these ideas to the subject. Preserve factual accuracy and distinguish illustration from evidence.

## Presentation options

For a film review page, consider a fitted aspect ratio, poster-to-playing transition, explicit sound controls, keyboard access and reduced-motion behavior.

A single-file HTML package is optional. Prefer separate media for long films to avoid base64 size and memory overhead. A live grain overlay must become seeded and time-based for repeatable video export.

## Example

For an illustrated lighthouse scene, establish the coastline, move attention to the unlit lamp, reveal its first beam, then return to the coast. The palette, pace and number of shots remain creative choices.

## Limits and source

Moss Keeper embeds a finished MP4; its HTML does not generate the character animation. The film-production method cannot be learned from this repository alone. Do not reuse its character or media by default.
